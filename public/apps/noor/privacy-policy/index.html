Noor Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026
App: Noor (Android)
Developer: Bilal Mahmood
Package: com.ghostinthetoast.noor

This policy applies to the Noor mobile application. It explains what information the app uses, why it is used, and how it is stored. Noor is a prayer-times and azaan utility. It does not require an account.

Information the app uses

  • Approximate and precise location. Used only to calculate local prayer times and to show a nearby city name. If location permission is denied or unavailable, the app falls back to Makkah coordinates.
  • App settings. Prayer-calculation method, Asr method, and the last location used for scheduling are stored on the device.
  • Notifications and exact alarms. Used to play the azaan at scheduled prayer times, including after the device restarts.

How information is stored

Location coordinates and settings are stored locally on your device (Android SharedPreferences). Noor does not operate its own servers and does not create a user account, profile, or cloud backup of this data.

Third parties

Reverse geocoding (turning coordinates into a city name) may use Google Play services / the Android geocoder. That request is limited to resolving a place name for display. Noor does not include ads, analytics SDKs, crash reporters, or social-login SDKs.

If you install Noor from Google Play, Google also processes install and store data under Google’s privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use your location for advertising.
  • We do not share your location with other apps or companies for their marketing.

Your choices

  • Deny or revoke location permission in Android settings. The app will use the Makkah fallback.
  • Disable notifications or exact-alarm permission. Prayer alerts will not fire.
  • Uninstall the app to remove locally stored settings and location.

Children

Noor is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new effective date.
